# PR description template (student rewrites in their own words)

**Fixes** #<issue>

**Problem**
What was going wrong, and how to see it (steps or failing test).

**Cause**
Why it happened, in one or two sentences.

**Change**
What you changed and why this approach. Mention anything you chose *not* to change.

**Testing**
- Test added: `<name>` (fails before, passes after)
- Commands run: `<exact commands>`
- Not verified: `<platforms or cases you could not test>`

**Notes for reviewers**
Anything surprising. Disclose AI assistance here if the project asks for it.
